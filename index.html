Schedule

Things We Can Do

Wednesday 16 September
Arrival
  • 🚕 Bolt / Uber Recommended 📍 Airport — Open the app at arrivals and order to Draggo House. 25–30 min, no faff. Best option for a group with bags. ~35–50 PLN / £7–10
  • 🚌 Bus 208 or 902 — From the airport bus stop to the city centre. ~50 min. Cheap but slower and you'll need to walk the last bit with luggage. ~6 PLN / £1.20
Day
  • 🏛️ Rynek Główny 📍 Map — 2 min walk. Main Market Square — grab a beer, catch the bugle call from St. Mary's on the hour. Free.
  • 🏰 Wawel Castle & Hill 🌐 Website — 10 min walk south. Castle, royal cathedral, dragon's cave at the base. ~40 PLN / £8pp
  • 🧱 Florian Gate & Barbican 📍 Map — 8 min walk north. Medieval fortifications. Quick to walk and impressive up close. Free.
Night
📍 Head to Old Town — everything is within 5 min walk of Draggo. Start at Pijalnia, then the night takes care of itself.
  • 🍻 Pijalnia Wódki i Piwa Start here 📍 Map — 4 min walk. The first stop on every great Kraków night. Shots and beers for next to nothing. ~4 PLN / 80p a shot
Also in the area
  • 🥃 Vodka Tasting 📍 Map — 4 min walk. Guided Polish vodka tasting — 5–6 varieties with commentary. ~50–70 PLN / £10–14pp
  • 🍺 ul. Floriańska 📍 Map — 3 min walk. Main bar street — walk it and see what takes your fancy.
  • 🌃 Shine Club 📍 Map — 5 min walk. Good for later when you want it louder. ~20 PLN / £4 entry
Thursday 17 September
Day
  • 🔫 Shooting Range — pick a venue
    Strzelnica Łobzów Our pick 📍 Map · 1.5km NW · 10 min taxi · Handguns, rifles, shotguns. Book a group package 24 hrs ahead. ~150–200 PLN / £30–40pp
    Combat Zone Kraków 📍 Map · 4km · 15 min taxi (transport often included via stag company) · More weapon variety. ~170–220 PLN / £34–44pp
or
  • 🏎️ Go Karting 📍 Map — 5km south, 15 min taxi. Indoor karting circuit. Book a group session — most tracks offer timed races with a leaderboard. ~80–120 PLN / £16–24pp
Night
📍 Head to Kazimierz — 15 min walk or 5 min taxi from Draggo. Start with the oil wrestling, then bar hop from Alchemia.
  • 💋 Female Oil Wrestling Start here 🌐 Book — Book through a Kraków stag company (e.g. Stag Krakow, The Stag Company). Private venue in Kazimierz — transport and entry sorted for you. ~£20–30pp
Also in the area
  • 🕯️ Alchemia 🌐 Website — Candlelit cellar bar. The iconic Kazimierz spot — great atmosphere, cold beer. ~12 PLN / £2.50 a beer
  • ☭ Propaganda 📍 Map — Communist-themed bar two minutes from Alchemia. Cheap drinks, gets lively by 10pm. ~8–12 PLN / £1.60–2.50 a beer
Friday 18 September
Day
  • ⛏️ Krakow Underground Museum 🌐 Website — 2 min walk. Excavated medieval market streets beneath Rynek. Good quick culture fix before the big night. ~25 PLN / £5pp
  • 🏭 Schindler's Factory 🌐 Website — 20 min walk / 10 min taxi. Best WWII museum in Poland. Genuinely moving. ~25 PLN / £5pp
  • 🎨 MOCAK 🌐 Website — 20 min walk / 10 min taxi (next to Schindler's). Modern art — lighter option if the war museum is heavy. ~15 PLN / £3pp

Saturday 19 September
Day
  • 🧂 Wieliczka Salt Mine 🌐 Website — 30 min by taxi. UNESCO site. Underground world carved from salt over 700 years, including a cathedral. Book online. ~99 PLN / £20pp
  • 🏰 Wawel Castle full tour 🌐 Website — 10 min walk. Staterooms, royal treasury, and armoury. Better with a guide. ~40–65 PLN / £8–13pp
  • 🏗️ Nowa Huta 📍 Map — 30 min by tram. Intact Soviet-era planned city. Completely different from Old Town, genuinely interesting. Free.
Night
📍 Head to Kazimierz for dinner — 15 min walk or 5 min taxi. Last night, 6am flight Sunday.
  • 🍽️ Starka Start here 📍 Map — 15 min walk / 5 min taxi. Classic Polish sit-down dinner. Good way to mark the end of the trip properly. ~60–80 PLN / £12–16pp
Also in the area
  • 🛋️ Mleczarnia 📍 Map — Two minutes from Starka. Cosy bar, low music, good beer. Ideal wind-down spot. ~12–15 PLN / £2.50–3 a beer
  • 🥣 Bar Mleczny 📍 Map — Old Town, 5 min walk from Draggo. Canteen-style Polish food. Cheap, filling, exactly what's needed after a big week. ~20–30 PLN / £4–6pp
Sunday 20 September
Morning
⏰ Flight departs 06:10. Aim to leave Draggo no later than 03:30 to allow time for check-in and security. Set multiple alarms.
  • 🚕 Bolt / Uber Recommended 📍 Airport — Book in advance or order on the app at 03:30. 25–30 min to the airport. Cheapest option for a group at that hour. ~35–50 PLN / £7–10
  • 🚖 Pre-booked taxi — If you want certainty at 3am, book a local Kraków taxi company the day before (e.g. Radio Taxi 919). They'll be waiting outside Draggo. ~50–70 PLN / £10–14

Local Essentials

How to be a Local

🍺 What to Drink
Beer

Żywiec

Poland's most iconic lager. Crisp, golden, always cold.

Beer

Tyskie

The other classic. Lighter and more sessionable than Żywiec.

Spirit

Żubrówka

Bison Grass Vodka. Smooth with a faint herbal, vanilla note. Try it neat or as a Tatanka — with cloudy apple juice.

Spirit

Soplica

Polish fruit vodka — cherry, plum, hazelnut and more. Sweet, smooth.

🍽️ What to Eat
Dish

Pierogi

The national dish. Stuffed dumplings — ruskie (potato and cheese) or meat are the classics.

Dish

Żurek

Sour rye soup served in a hollowed bread bowl with sausage and a hard-boiled egg.

Dish

Bigos

Hunter's stew. Sauerkraut, meat, mushrooms and herbs slow-cooked for hours. Deeply savoury.
