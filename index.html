Local Recommendations

Drop-In Options

Straight from a Kraków local — worth slotting in wherever there's a gap in the day. All of these are pinned on the map above.

🍴 Food
  • 🥟 Zielony Kredens 📍 Map — ul. Świętego Krzyża 3, Old Town. Cheap and tasty Polish cuisine, mostly pierogi. Good lunch menu Monday–Friday.
  • 🥪 Mr. Broda 📍 Map — ul. Krakowska 31, Kazimierz. Great pastrami sandwiches — they cure their own. Closed Mondays.
  • ⭐ Kropka 🌐 Michelin Guide — ul. Józefa Dietla 31. Michelin Guide modern cuisine, small plates to share. The nice one. ~£40pp
  • 🫓 Zapiekanki, Plac Nowy 📍 Map — Kazimierz. Traditional Polish fast food. A bit of a tourist trap, but cheap and part of the experience.
  • 🌭 Sausages from the blue "Nysa" 📍 Map — ul. Grzegórzecka 3. Sausages grilled over a fire out the back of an old blue van. Ideal between pubs. Open 7pm–3am, closed Sundays.
🍺 Old Pubs
  • 🎹 Harris Piano Jazz Bar 🌐 Website — Rynek Główny 28. Underground jazz cellar right on the main square. Live music every night.
  • 🕯️ Alchemia 🌐 Website — ul. Estery 5, Kazimierz. Candlelit cellar bar and the iconic Kazimierz spot.
  • 🪴 Eszeweria 🌐 Website — ul. Józefa 9, Kazimierz. Bohemian, mismatched furniture, back garden. Open until 2am.
🎭 Culture
  • 🖼️ Muzeum Czartoryskich 🌐 Website — ul. Pijarska 15, Old Town. Really nice museum, and home to Leonardo da Vinci's Lady with an Ermine. Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00. ~65 PLN / £13pp
  • 🕴️ Polonia Wax Museum 🌐 Website — ul. Floriańska 32. Famous for being bad. Roasted by Clarkson on The Grand Tour, which is exactly why it's worth it.
🥾 If You Fancy a Wander
  • 🏞️ Zakrzówek 📍 Map — Old limestone quarry flooded with turquoise water, ringed by cliffs. 20 min taxi southwest. Genuinely pretty.
  • ⛰️ Kopiec Krakusa 📍 Map — Ancient mound on Lasota Hill, Podgórze. 3km south. Short climb, best view over the city. Free.

Local Essentials

How to be a Local

🍺 What to Drink
Beer

Żywiec

Poland's most iconic lager. Crisp, golden, always cold.

Beer

Tyskie

The other classic. Lighter and more sessionable than Żywiec.

Spirit

Żubrówka

Bison Grass Vodka. Smooth with a faint herbal, vanilla note. Try it neat or as a Tatanka — with cloudy apple juice.

Spirit

Soplica

Polish fruit vodka — cherry, plum, hazelnut and more. Sweet, smooth.

🍽️ What to Eat
Dish

Pierogi

The national dish. Stuffed dumplings — ruskie (potato and cheese) or meat are the classics.

Dish

Żurek

Sour rye soup served in a hollowed bread bowl with sausage and a hard-boiled egg.

Dish

Bigos

Hunter's stew. Sauerkraut, meat, mushrooms and herbs slow-cooked for hours. Deeply savoury.
